THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Meshes for Visualization. Voxels for Measurement.

3D Mesh Visualization

Surface meshes reconstructed via Marching Cubes deliver fluid 60 FPS WebGL rendering for inspecting hepatic lobes, vessels, and lesion margins.

Voxel-based Volumetric Calculation

Physical Voxel Volume
Vvoxel = Δx · Δy · Δz
Remaining Volume
VRLV = Nrem · Vvoxel
Future Liver Remnant
FLR = VRLV / (VTLV − VT)

Every volumetric metric is computed directly on the calibrated 3D CT voxel lattice via analytical half-space plane partitioning, eliminating mesh-smoothing bias.

VALIDATION STRATEGY

Built with Accuracy in Mind.

Volume Consistency

Strict voxel conservation verified across all oblique resection plane angles with zero double-counting.

Synthetic Shape Testing

Benchmarked against closed-form analytical volumes (spheres, cubes, ellipsoids) under arbitrary plane slices.

Anisotropic Voxel Spacing

Preserves physical accuracy when axial pixel spacing differs from thick CT z-axis slices (Δz > Δx, Δy).

Tumor Exclusion

Boolean mask subtraction excludes non-functional tumor mass from total functional volume and FLR denominators.

Mesh–Voxel Alignment

Guarantees that the 3D resection plane manipulated in Three.js world space maps identically through the NIfTI affine transform matrix into the physical voxel coordinate lattice.

04 / Validation and Demonstration

Complete synthetic phantom testing, anisotropic spacing verification, and academic prototype demonstration.
